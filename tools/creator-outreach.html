Creator outreach tracker

Every YouTube creator embedded across the upshard roadmap lessons, filtered down to the smaller/niche channels worth a personal heads-up that their video was hand-picked as the canonical resource for a lesson node — plus a slightly larger "mid-size" tier if you want to widen the net.
How "small" was decided: every lesson's frontmatter names a creator and channel — that part is exact. The Small / Mid-size split is a heuristic based on general knowledge, not a live subscriber-count check (corporate brands, conferences, and well-known mega-channels like IBM Technology, 3Blue1Brown, freeCodeCamp, NeetCode, etc. are excluded entirely). Channels I didn't recognize at all defaulted to "small", which is usually a good sign for this purpose. Spot-check subscriber counts before messaging anyone you're unsure about.
